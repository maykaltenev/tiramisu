Coffee and rum

200 ml espresso

2 tbsp rum

Pour the espresso and rum into a shallow dish and let it cool. Each ladyfinger gets dipped in this.

Dip the biscuits

2 packages ladyfingers

Dip each ladyfinger in the coffee mix, then lay them side by side in the dish.

Whip the cream

500 g mascarpone

300 ml sweet cream

around 3 tbsp powdered sugar

Beat until half-whipped and smooth, then spread it over the biscuits.

Repeat

Add another layer of soaked biscuits, then more cream. Keep going for as many layers as you like.

Dust with cocoa

Finish with a layer of cream and sprinkle cocoa powder on top.

Ready to serve

  • 200 ml espresso + 2 tbsp rum
  • 2 packages ladyfingers
  • 500 g mascarpone
  • 300 ml sweet cream
  • around 3 tbsp powdered sugar
  • cocoa powder
